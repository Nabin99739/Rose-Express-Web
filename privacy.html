Privacy Policy

At Rose Express Cargo, we prioritize the protection of your personal and shipment data.

1. Information We Collect

To provide efficient logistics and courier services, we collect necessary personal information when you request a quote, book a shipment, or contact support. This includes:

  • Contact details (Name, Email Address, Phone Number).
  • Delivery and Collection Addresses.
  • Payment information (processed securely through certified gateways).
  • Shipment details (contents, value, and weight).

2. How We Use Your Information

The data collected is utilized exclusively to facilitate our core services:

  • To execute and manage courier services and deliveries.
  • To provide real-time tracking updates and notifications.
  • To process transactions and send invoices.
  • To improve customer support and respond to inquiries.

3. Data Security

We implement strict institutional protocols and technical safeguards to protect your shipment data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. Our tracking systems and databases use encrypted connections, ensuring that your logistical information remains confidential.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared strictly with trusted logistics partners (e.g., DHL, FedEx) and local carrier networks solely for the purpose of completing your delivery. These partners are bound by confidentiality agreements to protect your data.

5. Cookies

Our website utilizes essential cookies to ensure basic site functionality, such as maintaining your session during the tracking process and remembering your preferences. We do not use aggressive tracking cookies for third-party advertising.
